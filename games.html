Under the Hood

Not a game engine. A cognitive architecture.

Neurochemistry

7 chemicals (dopamine, serotonin, norepinephrine, oxytocin, GABA, cortisol, endorphins) drive all behavior. Emotions emerge from the Lovheim cube, not labels.

Global Workspace

Baars' Global Workspace Theory. 6 cognitive processors compete for consciousness. The winner broadcasts to all. This is how beings think.

TD Learning

Schultz dopamine model. Beings learn from prediction errors. Unexpected good = dopamine spike. Unexpected bad = dopamine dip. They genuinely learn.

Vasopressin Bonding

Sustained high oxytocin with another being deepens attachment. Bonds decay slower over time. Relationships have real biochemical weight.
